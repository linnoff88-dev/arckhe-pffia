The thesis

The serious focus for companies isn’t making pretty pages with AI. It’s redesign the work so humans can direct agents as they carry out increasingly large parts of the process. Anyone who knows how to do this with control, quality, and responsibility is one of the most valuable professionals from 2026 to 2028.

74%

of leaders expect that, in four years, almost half of the processes has been redesigned around AI agents (Deloitte).

5%

consider their own processes highly prepared today (Deloitte). The opportunity lies between the two numbers.

21%

of companies say they have mature governance for AI agents (Deloitte). Lesson 7 is about joining this group.

What you’ll know how to do

Results, not theory

Map a real process in six links (input, decision, action, verification, exception, result) and mark what can be delegated to agents.

Delegate goals, not tasks: write a long-horizon request with an approval checkpoint and test it in the AI chat you already use.

Build the orchestration chain: human, coordinator, specialists, tools, systems, verification, human.

Write the quality sheet and permissions matrix: what counts as the right answer, when to stop, when to call you, and what the agent cannot do.

Who it’s for
